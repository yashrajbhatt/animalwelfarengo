About Rise Above Ego
Founder

Jaskaran Singh

Director of Operations at Khwaahish NGO

Jaskaran Singh is the Director of Operations at Khwaahish NGO and the Founder of Rise Above Ego, an initiative focused on promoting personal growth, positive thinking, and the importance of overcoming ego to build better relationships and a stronger society. He pursued his Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) in Tax Law and Taxation from Kumaun University, Nainital. With his background in law, leadership, and social initiatives, Jaskaran Singh is committed to contributing to social welfare, empowering individuals, and creating a positive impact in the community.
